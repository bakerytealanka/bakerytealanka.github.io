Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful details about ingredients, storage, table bookings, and Southern Coast delivery.

Which towns do you deliver to on the Southern Coast?
We deliver daily from 09:00 to 20:30 across the entire Southern Coast: Hikkaduwa, Galle, Unawatuna, Thalpe, Ahangama, Kabalana, Midigama, Weligama, Mirissa, Matara, Hiriketiya, Dikwella, and Tangalle. Call +94 71 234 5678 or submit the form below to confirm delivery timing and fees.
How long do the matcha cashew truffles stay fresh?
Because we prepare them without artificial preservatives, store your truffles in the refrigerator in a closed container. They keep their crisp texture and flavor for up to 7 days.
Are your desserts vegetarian-friendly?
Yes, our entire pastry menu is vegetarian. The matcha cashew truffles are made without wheat flour (cashews, toasted coconut, local honey, and matcha powder), though they are prepared in a kitchen that also handles wheat, eggs, and dairy.
Do I need a reservation to visit the cafe?
Walk-in guests are welcome every day from 08:30 to 21:30. If you are visiting with 4 or more people or want to reserve a full matcha pound cake loaf, we recommend sending a quick request ahead of time.
